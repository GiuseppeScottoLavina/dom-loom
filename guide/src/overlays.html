DomLoom guide

Overlays

A modal <dialog> and an open popover live in the browser's top layer, above the page, where the canvas of a stage on the page cannot draw them. So an overlay gets a stage of its own: put a <loom-stage overlay> directly inside the native <dialog> or [popover]. That stage is transparent around its panel, and the native element keeps doing its job: focus, Escape, the backdrop, returnValue, light dismiss.

Dialogs

The panel opens from a line of light. A dialog closed by Escape, by a <form method="dialog"> or by a command="close" button shuts first, then closes with its returnValue. dialog.close() from a script closes it at once. Entry: dom-loom/dialog.

Open it and press Escape: it shuts, then closes. Open it again and hold Quit.

Drawers

overlay="left", "right", "top" or "bottom" opens the panel from that edge. In the default skin a left or right dialog is laid out as a full-height drawer.

Open the inventory: it slides in from the right edge. Close it, or press Escape.

Menus and popovers

A popover opened by a popovertarget button is anchored under it (above if there is no room below) and opens from that side. Inside it, a [role=menu] works as the ARIA menu pattern: the first item takes the focus; the arrow keys, Home, End and a letter move it while a light follows; menuitemcheckbox and menuitemradio items toggle their aria-checked; choosing an item closes the menu, and so do Escape and Tab; the focus returns to the button. Entries: dom-loom/popover, dom-loom/menu.

Open Options with the mouse, then again with the keyboard: Enter, the arrow keys, Escape.

A <select> needs none of this: a stage draws its list itself (see Controls).
