Ink, not light.

The survey card is a plain <form>: checkboxes, radios, a range, a text field and a submit button. One attribute puts the stage in the manga skin. Nothing glows: ink darkens the paper, every grey is a screentone, and the strong moments get the lines a manga panel would draw.

  • Pick how you read it. The selection is a reversed panel, white on ink, and the labels flip as it slides past.
  • Drag the hype. The fill is tone, and focus lines snap around the thumb while you hold it.
  • Tick a fourth series. The card keeps three, and says so.
  • Hold Send. Tone fills the button and it trembles. When it lands, the panel hits and the card turns over.
